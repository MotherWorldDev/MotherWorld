# Land pollution sources

## OpenStreetMap

- Waste-site tags: https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:landuse%3Dlandfill
- Regional PBF downloads: https://download.geofabrik.de/
- License: Open Database License (ODbL)

## Climate TRACE

- Data downloads: https://climatetrace.org/data
- Waste sector includes solid waste disposal on land, including landfill/dumpsite source-level estimates.
- Climate TRACE outputs are published under CC BY 4.0; its terms also identify upstream source datasets whose terms should be reviewed.

## Global-scale mining polygons v2

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.942325
- 44,929 polygons and 101,583 km² of mapped mining-related land in version 2.
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0.

## WAPHA global metal mines/tailings

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.j3tx95xmg
- Includes public downloadable tailings storage facility and tailings dam failure shapefiles.

## US EPA Superfund

- https://www.epa.gov/superfund/superfund-data-and-reports
- EPA publishes current geospatial information for proposed/final/deleted NPL and Superfund Alternative Approach sites.

## US EPA Toxic Release Inventory

- https://www.epa.gov/toxics-release-inventory-tri-program/tri-basic-data-files-calendar-years-1987-present
- Basic files include facility coordinates and on-site land disposal/release quantities by chemical.

## European Industrial Emissions Portal / E-PRTR

- https://industry.eea.europa.eu/industrial-emissions/dataset
- Current industrial reporting includes facility locations, pollutant releases and regulated waste transfers.
- MotherWorld uses the user-friendly `F4_2` detailed facility waste-transfer export when staged.
