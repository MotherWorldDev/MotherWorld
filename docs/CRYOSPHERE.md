# Cryosphere family

Global-only family.

The turnkey component uses NOAA/NSIDC Sea Ice Index v4 annual minimum extent for both hemispheres and scores retention relative to the 1981–2010 mean annual minimum. Optional staged Greenland, Antarctic, and mountain-glacier time series are retained as context in v1; they should become scored components after their reference/critical normalization is agreed.

Current family coverage is therefore deliberately 60%, so Earth Health knows the cryosphere module is incomplete rather than treating missing land-ice components as healthy.

## Earth Health historical backbone (v3)

For fixed-composition Earth Health history, Cryosphere is scored only from the long-running NOAA/NSIDC sea-ice backbone (Arctic and Antarctic annual-minimum retention). Ice-sheet and glacier products remain present-day diagnostics until a separate versioned Earth Health methodology deliberately promotes them. This supersedes the older v1 coverage-weight interpretation above for the Earth Health headline.
