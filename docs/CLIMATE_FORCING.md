# Climate forcing family

Global-only family. NOAA's Annual Greenhouse Gas Index (AGGI) is the scored headline because it already combines radiative forcing from long-lived greenhouse gases. Global annual CO₂, CH₄, and N₂O remain visible context.

The default MotherWorld normalization is transparent and configurable: AGGI 1.0 (1990 forcing reference) maps to 100; AGGI 2.0 maps to 0. This is a MotherWorld condition scale, **not an official NOAA health threshold**.

## Earth Health historical backbone (v3)

AGGI is the sole scored Climate Forcing backbone in the fixed Earth Health history. Individual CO2, CH4 and N2O series remain explanatory diagnostics and therefore cannot change the historical/current formula.
