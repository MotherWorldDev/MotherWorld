# Identified next global-system families

Freshwater Stability is now implemented as a global-only family.

Still intentionally not scored in this bundle because their normalization/reference choices are not yet locked:

- **Biogeochemical nutrient cycles** — nitrogen and phosphorus loading/surplus
- **Nutrient cycles** — total-column ozone and ozone-depleting-gas burden

Potential future Freshwater drill-down is hydrological rather than ecoregional: basin/river/lake views may later use HydroBASINS, GloFAS and the larger lakes/rivers geometry set. This does not change the global Freshwater Stability family.
