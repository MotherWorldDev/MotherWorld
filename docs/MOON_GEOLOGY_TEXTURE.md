# USGS Moon geology texture

Source: Corey M. Fortezzo, Paul D. Spudis and Shannon L. Harrel (2020), *Unified Geologic Map of the Moon, 1:5,000,000*, USGS Astrogeology Science Center, version 2.

- Global coverage: -180..180 longitude, -90..90 latitude
- Lunar stratigraphic keywords include Copernican, Eratosthenian, Imbrian, Nectarian and Pre-Nectarian
- Use constraints: CC0; cite the authors
- Official product page: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/Moon/Geology/Unified_Geologic_Map_of_the_Moon_GIS_v2/

The MotherWorld builder expects the published global colored raster to be approximately 2:1 and converts it to browser-friendly WebP without labels, poster margins or map furniture.
