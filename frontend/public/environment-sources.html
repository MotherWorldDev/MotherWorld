MotherWorld environmental sources and scoring

MotherWorld separates source measurements, 0–100 normalization, family indices, data coverage, and the global Earth Health index. A missing measurement is never interpreted as healthy condition.

Earth Health history: the headline and year slider use one fixed set of nine long-running backbones from 1993 to the latest year shared by every backbone. Newer/richer datasets are present-day diagnostics only and have zero Earth Health weight.
Score direction: 100 = best condition / lowest pressure. Missing data never counts as healthy.

Regional Climate tab

The existing temperature-distribution module is supplemented by a separate 1991–2020 climatology for precipitation, relative humidity and wind. Land/lake regions use ECMWF ERA5-Land Daily Aggregated; marine regions use ECMWF ERA5 Daily. Relative humidity is derived from daily-mean temperature and dew point. Wind uses daily-mean 10 m u/v components and a 16-sector wind rose. These regional climate charts are diagnostics and carry zero Earth Health weight.

The open_ocean visual selection follows the MotherWorld ocean rule: species remain residual-only, while precipitation/humidity/wind describe the complete ocean system using a fractional ocean mask.

Geology tab

Geology is descriptive and has zero Earth Health weight. Land geology uses GLiM, optional Macrostrat, USGS MRDS, GEM active faults, Smithsonian GVP, IHFC/GFZ heat flow and confirmed-impact context. Impact records preserve source-supplied age uncertainty and impactor estimates. Marine Geology additionally supports GEBCO_2026 bathymetry, EarthByte/Seton seafloor age and spreading, GlobSed sediment thickness, optional CRUST1.0 crystalline-crust thickness, EarthByte/GPlates ridge/trench features and InterRidge hydrothermal vents. The Global Ocean selection aggregates these non-species metrics over the whole ocean.

Moon crater context: Robbins (2019), archived by USGS Astrogeology / NASA PDS, catalogs 1,296,879 lunar impact craters ≥1 km and 6,972 ≥20 km; completeness is estimated for craters larger than roughly 1–2 km.

Global Ocean scope

The selectable open_ocean region is the visual residual outside mapped marine/coastal ecoregions. Species remain scoped to that residual. Every non-species Global Ocean metric represents the complete ocean system: residual + mapped coastal/marine regions. Native whole-ocean provider values are preferred when available.

Licensing firewall

Raw restricted inputs such as NHM BII and IUCN spatial downloads stay outside the deployed tree. The public site receives only permitted derived regional components/indices. Providers are removable without changing the public family-index schema.
